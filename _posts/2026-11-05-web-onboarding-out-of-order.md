---
title: Registering in "Antares Web" currently out of order
---

Certain features (like "Register" or "Forgot Password") in Antares Web that involve sending emails are currently out of order.

The reason for this is an issue with railway.com, the provider that hosts the Antares backend. They changed a configuration such that sending emails the way the Antares backend does is no longer possible.

We are working on a solution to this issue.
